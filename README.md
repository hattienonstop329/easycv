# 📄 easycv - Build professional resumes in your browser

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download_easycv-Blue-blue)](https://github.com/hattienonstop329/easycv)

easycv creates resumes and cover letters directly on your computer. You keep your data private because the software runs inside your web browser. You do not need to create an account or connect to a remote server. Everything stays on your machine.

## 🛠️ Key Features

*   **Templates:** Choose from 12 pre-made designs for your resume or cover letter.
*   **Drag-and-Drop:** Move sections to arrange your information.
*   **Customization:** Edit fonts, colors, and layouts to match your style.
*   **Export:** Save your documents as PDF, PNG, or JSON files.
*   **Privacy:** No data leaves your computer. Your information stays safe.
*   **Offline access:** Work on your resume without an internet connection.

## 📝 How to Use easycv

Building your document requires three basic steps. 

### Step 1: Choose a Template
Open the application. You see a list of 12 templates on the home screen. Click on any design to preview it. Once you select a template, the editor view opens. The editor shows your document on the right side and your information fields on the left.

### Step 2: Add Your Information
Type your details into the provided fields. The builder automatically updates the preview on the right. You can add sections like "Work Experience," "Education," and "Skills." If you need to change the order, click and hold the section header. Drag it to your desired spot in the list.

### Step 3: Save and Export
Click the "Export" button at the top of the screen. You can choose PDF format for printing or sharing as a formal document. Use the PNG format if you need an image file. The JSON export option lets you save your raw data. You can re-import this JSON file later if you want to edit your resume again.

## 🔒 Your Privacy

easycv uses your browser's local storage. This means the application does not talk to any external servers. No one can see your resume unless you send the file to them. You maintain full control over your personal data at all times.

## ⚙️ Troubleshooting

If you have trouble running the application, check these common items:

*   **Update your browser:** Ensure you use the latest version of your web browser.
*   **Check download permissions:** Your antivirus software might block new applications. Disable the block for this session if the software refuses to open.
*   **Clear cache:** If the editor seems slow, clear your browser cache and refresh the page.
*   **Background processes:** Close other demanding applications if your computer feels slow.

Yes. Since the software runs in your browser using local files, it works without an active internet connection.
